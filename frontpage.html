PVO Chatbot

AI Assistant

An AI assistant built directly into the platform. Ask it anything — about volunteering, navigating the site, or how to get started — and it responds instantly in plain language, no experience required.

About the Platform

Built with purpose, for those who served

Open Coding Society is a student-run organization at Poway High School. Everything on the PVO platform was conceived, designed, and shipped by students.

💡

Student-Built

Every feature was designed and developed by students building real software engineering skills through hands-on, project-based work.

🎖️

Real Impact

These aren't just school projects. They solve genuine problems — connecting veterans with help, enabling volunteers, and making AI accessible.

🔓

Open & Accessible

Good tools should be free and open. Everything on the PVO platform is available to anyone in the community at no cost.
