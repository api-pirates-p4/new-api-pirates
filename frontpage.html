Features

Three tools worth exploring

01  ·  AI Assistant

PVO Chatbot

An AI assistant built into the platform. Ask it anything — about volunteering, the site, or how to get started — and it responds instantly in plain language, no matter your experience level.

Why it matters: Not everyone knows where to start. The chatbot removes that friction, making the whole platform more accessible to anyone who visits.

Open Chatbot
02  ·  Machine Learning

MelTitanic

An interactive machine learning demo built around the Titanic dataset. Enter passenger details and see a real ML model predict survival outcomes — a hands-on way to understand how algorithms work.

Why it matters: Machine learning can feel abstract. MelTitanic makes it tangible by grounding it in a story people know, turning a complex topic into something you can actually play with.

Try the demo
03  ·  Community

Volunteer Hub

A simple place to find, sign up for, and track volunteer opportunities. It connects students who want to give back with the events and causes that need them.

Why it matters: Community service is a big part of what Open Coding Society stands for. This tool makes it easy to act on that — turning good intentions into real, organized effort.
