Platform Tools

Three tools built for real impact

Each tool was designed and developed by students to solve genuine problems — from navigating AI conversations to understanding veteran eligibility.

01  ·  AI Assistant

AI Assistant

PVO Chatbot

An AI assistant built directly into the platform. Ask it anything — about volunteering, navigating the site, or how to get started — and it responds instantly in plain language, no experience required.

Why it matters: Not everyone knows where to begin. The chatbot removes that friction and makes the platform accessible to any visitor.

Open Chatbot
02  ·  Fast Prescreener

Fast Prescreener

Veteran Eligibility Prescreener

Veterans can instantly find out whether they qualify for PVO support — no lengthy forms, no waiting. Enter a few details and our trained machine learning model provides an immediate eligibility result.

Why it matters: Powered by real data, this tool gives veterans a clear, immediate answer about whether PVO can help them — so the right people get connected to resources faster.

Check eligibility
03  ·  Community

Community

Volunteer Hub

A dedicated space to find, sign up for, and track volunteer opportunities. It connects students who want to give back with the events and causes that need them most.

Why it matters: Community service is central to what Open Coding Society stands for. This tool turns good intentions into organized, real-world effort.

About the Platform

Built with purpose

Open Coding Society is a student-run organization at Poway High School. Everything on the PVO platform was conceived, designed, and shipped by students.

💡

Student-built

Every feature was designed and developed by students building real software engineering skills through hands-on, project-based work.

🌱

Real impact

These aren't just school projects. They solve genuine problems — connecting veterans with help, enabling volunteers, and making AI accessible.

🔓

Open and accessible
